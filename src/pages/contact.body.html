Can you remove scratches with the fish still in the tank?

Yes. We specialize in removing scratches off your aquarium with or without water, 100% fish and coral safe. The materials we use to polish acrylic have been used for over 20 years in the aquarium industry.

Do you offer emergency services?

We provide 24/7 emergency services, temporary holding tanks and life support systems. Call us at +1 (562) 566-0150.

Can you move my aquarium to a new location?

Our team will help you move your aquarium from point A to point B. We also provide temporary holding tanks and 24/7 fish support systems.

What areas do you serve?

Southern California and nationwide.

Do you ship glass tanks?

Glass tanks need to ship by freight; there is no free shipping. See all tanks on our Glass Tanks page.

What warranty comes with a glass aquarium?

Tanks are warranted against leaks and cracks from defects in materials or workmanship for 6 months from purchase, and against leakage caused by sealant failure for 2 years. Read the full warranty.
